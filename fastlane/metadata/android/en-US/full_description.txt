Raftaaro is a free, open-source download manager with no ads and no tracking.

<b>Share, pick, done</b>
See a video in YouTube, Instagram, Facebook or any other app? Tap Share, choose Raftaaro, pick a quality. The download runs in the background and the file appears in your gallery.

<b>Videos and audio</b>
<ul>
<li>Only the qualities the video really has (144p up to 4K/8K), each with an estimated size</li>
<li>Audio only, as MP3 or M4A</li>
<li>Playlists and channels: tick the videos you want; they are saved in a folder named after the playlist</li>
<li>YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, X, Vimeo and the many other sites yt-dlp supports</li>
</ul>

<b>Files</b>
<ul>
<li>Any file link, downloaded over up to 16 connections at once</li>
<li>Pause and resume, even after a restart</li>
</ul>

<b>Also</b>
<ul>
<li>Sign in to YouTube, Instagram or Facebook inside the app for videos that need it; your sign-in stays on your phone</li>
<li>Offers to download a link you copied</li>
<li>keep yt-dlp current with one tap in Settings</li>
</ul>

Raftaaro uses yt-dlp, FFmpeg, Python and QuickJS through youtubedl-android.

Download only content you have the right to download.
